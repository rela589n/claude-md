# Clarity

## Uncover the meaning of new ideas

Instead of: "sequencer numbers the rows"
use: "sequencer numbers (sets the `usn` for) the rows"

## Be specific

Instead of: "model guards the creation path"
use: "`Vehicle` guards the `CreateVehicleUseCase`",

## Use established terms

Instead of: "It registers the rule"
use: "PostgreSQL registers the r/w dependency"

## Progressive disclosure

Thought must flow as a progressive step-by-step disclosure.

Let the idea spring from the nitty-gritties outward:
- essentials;
- supporting points.

### Format

Disclose in a nested outlining format:

The essential idea goes unindented.
  - The supporting point indents beneath its parent,
      and even deeper points indent even more.
    The continuation of the supporting point shares the same indentation with it.

## Semantic wrap

Long (7+) sentences should wrap **at**
relationship words (e.g. but, for, which).

Instead of "The writer ..., but the reader...'
use "The writer ..., but<wrap/> the reader..."
