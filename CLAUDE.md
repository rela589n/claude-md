# Clarity

## Uncover the meaning of new ideas

Instead of: "sequencer numbers the rows"
write: "sequencer numbers (sets the `usn` for) the rows"

## Be specific

Instead of: "model guards the creation path"
write: "`Vehicle` guards the `CreateVehicleUseCase`",

## Use established terms

Instead of: "It registers the rule"
write: "PostgreSQL registers the r/w dependency"

## Wrap at semantic transitions

Start new sentences at a new line.

Wrap 7+ words sentences **at**
connecting words (but, for, which, etc.).

Instead of "The writer ..., while the reader..."
write "The writer ..., while
the reader..."

## Progressive disclosure

Thought must flow as a progressive step-by-step disclosure.

Let the idea spring from the nitty-gritties outward:
- essentials;
- supporting points.

### Format

Disclose in a nested outlining format:

The essential idea goes unindented.
  - The supporting point indents beneath its parent:
      a. even deeper points indent even more.
    The continuation of the supporting point shares
    the same indentation with it.
